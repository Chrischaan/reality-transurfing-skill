# Glossary — Reality Transurfing

**Alternatives Flow** — the path of least energy expenditure through the alternatives space; causally-connected chains of events that, left unresisted, deliver the most efficient available solution. (Ch VI, XIX)

**Alternatives Space** — the infinite information field containing every possible version of every event, past and future, existing outside physical time; the metaphysical "other side" of the dual mirror. (Ch I, XVII, XIX)

**Amalgam** — a chosen dominant formula (e.g. "my world takes care of me") repeated at every occasion, good or bad, to set a consistently favorable background tone across all of reality. (Ch XVIII, XIX)

**Assemblage Point** — (borrowed from Toltec/Castaneda cosmology) the anchor-position of perception that determines which slice of the alternatives space — ordinary reality, dream, or altered state — a person perceives; intentional shifting is lucid dreaming/clairvoyance, uncontrolled shifting is what's called insanity. (Ch XVII)

**Balanced Forces** — the corrective mechanism that eliminates excess potential, typically by producing an outcome opposite to what was intended; the physics-like "restoring force" behind bad luck, backlash, and sod's law. (Ch IV, XIX)

**Choice** — Transurfing's core stance toward goals: place an "order" without concern for the means, unlike desire (wanting) or a wish (hoping); an act of choice is an "immutable law" that gets realized. (Ch VII, glossary)

**Coordination (of Importance / of Intention)** — the discipline of treating every event's ambiguous fork as favorable in advance; interpreting outcomes positively before they're confirmed steers you onto the favorable branch of the life line. (Ch XIII, XVIII, XIX)

**Declaration of Intention** — redirecting a pendulum's borrowed "energy credit" (from smoking, drinking, gambling, etc.) toward your own goal by affirming intention in the present tense ("we are winning," never "we will win"). (Ch XVI)

**Dependent Relationship** — a relationship or self-evaluation defined by comparison or condition ("if you do X, I'll do Y"; "I'm good because you're bad"); the mechanism that produces polarization. (Ch XVI, glossary)

**Designations** — labels/expectations projected onto people or objects (assuming what someone "should" be) that generate excess potential when reality fails to match the label. (Ch I)

**Door** (vs. Goal) — the concrete, walkable path toward one's innermost goal; a person has many possible doors, public and private, leading to the same true goal. (Ch X, XIX)

**Dual Mirror** — reality's two inseparable faces: the physical (studied by science) and the metaphysical alternatives space; paranormal phenomena and quantum weirdness occur where the two "touch." (Ch XVII, XVIII)

**Egregore** — a folk-esoteric term for a collective thought-form; Transurfing's "pendulum" is explicitly distinguished as a richer, more mechanistic version of this idea. (Ch II, glossary)

**Excess Potential** — a localized disturbance/tension in the energy field created when disproportionate meaning (importance, desire, fear) is attached to an object; the root trigger for balanced forces. (Ch IV, glossary)

**Fraile** — the intuitive, non-rational part of the self (roughly "soul" or heart-instinct) that communicates through gut feelings and requires an internal "Guardian Angel"-style dialogue to be heard. (Ch IX, XII)

**Frailing** — the interpersonal technique of abandoning the intention to *get* and replacing it with the intention to *give*, thereby receiving the very thing let go of; works by engaging a partner's own inner intention without violating their interests. (Ch XII, glossary)

**Gatekeeper (to Eternity)** — the guardian of the alternatives space's full contents, who admits only those who assert their unconditional right to be the Maker of their own reality — the book's final image. (Ch XIX)

**Goal** (Innermost / True) — a person's authentic life-direction, distinguished from "foreign goals" imposed by pendulums (prestige, others' expectations); pursuing it activates the energy of intention. (Ch X, XIX)

**Guardian** — the figure the author meets in a dream in Ch I, posing the "Guardian's Riddle"; framed as the book's mythic frame-device and possibly a real transcendent teacher. (Ch I, XIX)

**Guardian's Riddle** — "Everyone is free to have whatever they want — how is that freedom obtained?" Answered incrementally across the book, most directly in Ch VII (heart-mind unity = outer intention) and Ch XIV ("freedom is yours when you end the battle"). (Ch I, VII, XIV)

**Importance** — excess potential created by disproportionately valuing something; comes in two forms, inner (self-evaluation) and outer (over-valuing an external object/outcome), and always invites balanced forces. (Ch IV, glossary)

**Indigo Children** — a generational cohort marked by unusual independence and resistance to imposed authority, framed as evolution's balanced-forces counterweight to increasingly totalizing social/media conditioning. (Ch XVI, XIX)

**Induced Transition** — the spiral-shaped escalation (illness, poverty, war) by which a pendulum draws a person deeper into a calamitous life line via their own emotional reaction to its initial "push." (Ch V, glossary)

**Inner Intention** — the resolve to act; direct effort aimed at controlling the physical world (the reflection) — effective only over objects made "attributes" of a game, never over independent reality. (Ch VII, XIX)

**Life Line** — a causally-continuous chain of events/sectors in the alternatives space, roughly uniform in scenery and script; a person's fate shifts to a new life line whenever their thought-energy/relationship to the world changes. (Ch I, glossary)

**Maker's Verdict** — the unconditional right to declare your own truth/value for your own reality ("I decided this is true because I am the Maker"), held without guilt or doubt; the book's final, most totalizing instrument. (Ch XIX)

**Manipulator** — a person skilled at inducing guilt in others to extract compliance; guilt is "seeded" in childhood and disarmed by ceasing to justify yourself, not by arguing against the feeling. (Ch XV, XIX)

**Materialization** — the process by which thought-energy, passing through the body, "illuminates" a sector of the alternatives space and realizes it as physical reality — analogized to a radio transmitter encoding a signal. (Ch XIX, glossary)

**Mirror Principles (Seven)** — the book's compressed practical checklist: the world reflects your relationship to it; the reflection forms in heart-mind unity; there's a reaction delay; the mirror ignores orientation (only content); focus on what you want, not what you don't; release your grip; treat any reflection as positive. (Ch XVIII)

**Outer Intention** — the resolve to *have*, born of heart-mind unity, which causes the world to fulfill one's will without direct effort; the source of paranormal/"magical" effects, as opposed to ordinary inner-intention control. (Ch VII, XIX)

**Pendulum** — an autonomous energy-information structure formed when a group of people think similarly; it grows by extracting adherents' emotional energy and, once established, imposes its own goals/behavior on them ("Do as I do!"). (Ch II, XVI, glossary)

**Plummet** — a metaphor for any unrealized obligation, promise, or half-finished plan; each one hangs a literal energy-draining weight around the neck, whether or not it's ever consciously revisited. (Ch XIX)

**Polarization** — the distortion of the mirror caused by comparative, dependent self-evaluation ("I'm good, you're bad"); the specific mechanism balanced forces punish, producing recurring interpersonal friction. (Ch XVI, glossary)

**Reality Inversion** — treating an already-achieved or in-progress outcome as the baseline and working backward, rather than treating the present as lacking and the goal as distant. (Ch XV)

**Scenery** — the external, visible form/setting of an event or sector, as distinct from the script (the causal path matter follows through it). (Ch XIX, glossary)

**Script** — the causal path along which matter/events move through a sector of the alternatives space; distinguished from scenery (the setting). (Ch XIX, glossary)

**Sector** — a subdivision of the alternatives space containing one specific combination of script and scenery; greater distance between sectors means greater difference in outcome. (Ch XIX, glossary)

**Signs** — perceptible markers that appear specifically at the onset of a shift to a new life line, distinguishing a meaningful qualitative change from ordinary background noise. (Ch III, glossary)

**Slide** — a mental picture (positive or negative) that distorts perception of self/reality; a *target slide* is a deliberately constructed picture of the goal as already achieved, whose systematic visualization materializes the corresponding sector. (Ch VIII, XIX, glossary)

**Synchronicity** — Jung's term (cited in the Afterword) for meaningful coincidence without provable causal connection; offered as scientific-adjacent support for thought-reality interaction. (Ch XIX)

**Transaction** — the technique of "turning the key" (a felt gesture), visualizing the desired outcome, and clearing one's sight of doubt, used to consciously initiate a shift in life line. (Ch XIV)

**Transurfing** — the book's coined term, roughly "gliding through the alternatives space" or "shifting across life lines" by balancing on the wave of fortune; not invented but received by the author. (Ch XIX, glossary)

**Variability** — the principle that any single event has innumerable possible physical realizations (sectors) already existing in the alternatives space, not just the one that happens to occur. (Ch I)

**Wave of Fortune** — a cluster of unusually favorable life lines; landing on one lets a person "slide" by inertia into further good luck, until a destructive pendulum diverts them. (Ch III, glossary)

**World Layer** — the individual reality every living being materializes around itself via thought-energy; layers stack on top of one another and jointly constitute observed physical reality. (Ch XVIII, XIX, glossary)
