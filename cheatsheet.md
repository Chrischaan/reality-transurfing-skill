# Cheatsheet — Reality Transurfing

A reasoning aid for applying the framework to a real situation. Not a keyword list — use this to decide *which* mechanism is actually at play before reaching for a technique.

## First: What kind of problem is this?

| Symptom | Likely mechanism | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| "Everything is going wrong today," petty misfortunes piling up | Pendulum exacerbating conflict (jester jumping) while you're already stressed | [[patterns#Handling Pendulums]] — disengage or declare intention |
| Recurring friction with the same person/type of person | Polarization from comparative judgment | [[patterns#Diagnosing a Problem]] — polarization check |
| You want something badly and it keeps *not* happening | Excess potential / importance triggering balanced forces | Reduce importance before pushing harder — pushing harder is the trap |
| Low motivation, flat affect, nothing feels worth doing | Free-energy deficit, not physiological — check plummets/toxins first | [[patterns#Reducing Excess Potential / Freeing Energy]] |
| A goal feels like duty/tension rather than desire | Possibly a foreign goal imposed by a pendulum | [[patterns#Diagnosing a Problem]] — Foreign-Goal Checklist |
| Someone (child, partner, employee) resists your control | Over-tightened spokes / excess coercion | [[patterns#Interpersonal Techniques]] — Correcting the Mirror |
| You're chasing a result and monitoring it anxiously | Chasing your own reflection instead of shaping the image | Stop checking; the mirror has a delay — see Seven Mirror Principles |
| Guilt, inferiority, or fear/doubt is the actual obstacle | "Junk" cleared via feedback-chain cures, not direct argument | [[patterns#Reducing Excess Potential / Freeing Energy]] |

## Decision rule: Inner vs. Outer Intention

- Is the target something you can physically manipulate (an "attribute" — your own effort, a made object, a task)? → **Inner intention** is appropriate; direct action works.
- Is the target another person's will, an uncertain outcome, or "reality" broadly? → **Inner intention will fail or backfire.** Switch to **outer intention**: form the image, let go of the mechanism, let the alternatives flow deliver it.
- Diagnostic: if you notice yourself gripping, forcing, or repeating effort with no traction, that's the signal you're applying inner intention to something that only responds to outer intention.

## Decision rule: Which visualization technique to use

No technique in the Six Techniques for Working the Target Slide (frame, explosion wave, energy currents, outer sphere, scenery reduction, goal amalgam) outranks another. Choose by:
1. **Do you enjoy it?** If a technique feels like a chore, it will generate excess potential and undercut itself — switch to the goal amalgam ("for the lazy") instead.
2. **Is the goal near or far?** Near/concrete goals respond well to the frame + energy currents. Diffuse or long-horizon goals respond better to scenery reduction (projecting the slide over daily life) and the amalgam.
3. **Never combine effort with anxiety about results.** Any technique paired with checking-for-results defeats itself via the mirror's response delay.

## Threshold: When has importance gone too far?

Rule of thumb from Ch IV/XIX: if losing the object of desire/fear would feel *catastrophic* rather than merely disappointing, importance is already high enough to be generating excess potential. The intensity of the anticipated loss — not the objective size of the goal — is the signal.

## Threshold: Guilt/doubt/fear — is the Maker's Verdict blocked?

The Maker's Verdict ("I decide this is true because I am the Maker") only holds power when asserted **without hedging**. Test: if you notice yourself internally qualifying the verdict ("...I think," "...probably," "...unless"), the verdict has already collapsed back into "the accused" position — the fix is not more force, but locating and clearing the specific guilt/doubt first (see feedback-chain cures).

## Sequencing rule: energy before technique

Per Ch XI/XIX, visualization techniques and the amalgam are ineffective if free-energy reserves are already drained by plummets (unrealized obligations) or physiological toxins. **Always check energy levels before diagnosing a technique as "not working."** A technique failing repeatedly is more often an energy problem than a technique problem.

## When NOT to apply Transurfing

- To deliberately harm another person's reality/outcomes — outer intention only reliably shapes *your own* world layer, not someone else's (see Vanga case study, Ch XVIII).
- As a substitute for necessary physical-world action on your own goal — the flow supplies doors/opportunities, not the walking through them.
- As literal metaphysics to be defended — per the Afterword, treat the alternatives-space model as a useful scaffold, not a claim to defend against alternative explanations.
